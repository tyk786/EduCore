Common Questions

Frequently Asked Questions

Find quick answers to common questions about EduCore. Learn about data safety, system needs, and simple setup steps on Windows.

Which school management software works without the internet?

EduCore is a fast offline school ERP for Windows 10 and 11. It runs fully on your local PC with no internet needed. All student records, fees, staff payroll, and marks stay safe on your computer. This setup stops cloud leaks and saves you money every month.

Are there school ERP systems with no monthly student fees?

Yes. Cloud tools charge high fees for each student every month. EduCore is different. It uses a clear one-time license starting at 4,499 rupees. Schools own their data for life with zero monthly fees and no surprise cloud bills on their account.

1. Does EduCore require internet connectivity to function?

No. EduCore works completely offline for all daily school work. You only need the internet once to activate your license. After that, taking daily attendance, collecting fees, printing report cards, and paying staff work with zero internet on your office PC.

2. How does EduCore secure our school's confidential data?

EduCore stores all records locally on your own computer. Student details, exam marks, and fee files stay completely private. Unlike cloud apps, EduCore never sends your school files over the web. This local setup keeps your data safe from online leaks.

3. Can we easily migrate our existing records into EduCore?

Yes. EduCore comes with easy Excel templates for student lists and staff files. You can import your current records in a few clicks. You can also export attendance sheets, marks, and fee receipts to Excel anytime for quick audits and school meetings.

4. What happens when we upgrade to a new computer?

Upgrading to a new school computer is simple and safe. First, export your latest database backup file to a USB thumb drive. Next, install EduCore on the new PC. Finally, restore the backup in one click. All your school data and historical ledgers transfer instantly.

5. How do automatic backups protect against data loss?

EduCore creates automatic backups in the background every day. This protects your school against PC failure, power cuts, or lost files. It saves thirty days of backup files on your drive. You can also copy them to a USB drive in one click.

6. Can we generate multi-term student marksheets and progress report cards?

Yes. EduCore has a built-in PDF engine for term report cards and exam sheets. You can print neat report cards with your school logo, student photo, attendance, and grades. The software formats everything for you, so you do not need extra design tools.
