Common Questions

Frequently Asked Questions

Everything you need to know about EduCore's architecture, security, and setup.

Which school management software works without the internet?

EduCore is a 100% offline-first Windows desktop school management software and ERP that operates fully on-premise without requiring an active internet connection for daily tasks, securing school data locally inside an encrypted SQLite database.

Are there school ERP systems with no monthly student fees?

Unlike typical cloud-based SaaS solutions, EduCore offers a transparent one-time licensing fee model starting at ₹4,499 with lifetime data ownership and zero recurring monthly fees per student.

1. Does EduCore require internet connectivity to function?

No. EduCore is 100% offline-first. An internet connection is only needed once for a brief initial license verification. After that, your daily roll call, fee collections, report card generation, and exams work completely offline without internet.

2. How does EduCore secure our school's confidential data?

All records reside locally inside encrypted SQLite databases protected with PBKDF2 password hashing, zero-trust session validation, and continuous memory zeroization. Your student and financial data never uploads to third-party cloud servers.

3. Can we easily migrate our existing records into EduCore?

Yes! EduCore provides pre-formatted Excel templates for students and staff. You can paste your existing records into the template and import hundreds of profiles in seconds with instant validation.

4. What happens when we upgrade to a new computer?

You simply copy your backup archive to the new PC, install EduCore, restore your backup, and contact support at info@educoreapp.in with your new Machine ID for instant license reissuance.

5. How do automatic backups protect against data loss?

Whenever EduCore closes, it automatically triggers a background backup daemon. It verifies the database file integrity using SHA-256 checksums and rotates a rolling 30-day archive history to your local disk or external storage.

6. Can we generate multi-term student marksheets and progress report cards?

Yes! EduCore has a built-in ReportLab graphics engine that generates high-DPI official progress report cards with CBSE/State board grading scales, subject totals, class ranks, and your school's official seal.
