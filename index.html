Which school management software works without the internet?

EduCore is a fast offline school ERP for Windows 10 and 11. It runs fully on your local PC with no internet needed. All student records, fees, staff payroll, and marks stay safe on your computer. This setup stops cloud leaks and saves you money every month.

Are there school ERP systems with zero monthly student fees?

Yes. Cloud tools charge high fees for each student every month. EduCore is different. It uses a clear one-time license starting at 4,499 rupees. Schools own their data for life with zero monthly fees and no surprise cloud bills on their account.

What core administrative modules are included?

EduCore includes ten full modules in every plan. You get student records, daily attendance, fee receipts, and staff payroll. It also handles exam report cards, library books, school buses, and student ID cards. Every license comes with free daily backups on your PC.

What Windows operating systems are supported?

EduCore runs on 64-bit Windows 10 and Windows 11 computers. It needs at least 4 GB RAM and 500 MB free disk space. The app opens fast and works without lag. School staff can work smoothly all day with complete privacy on their local PC.

How does EduCore protect student records from computer failure?

EduCore creates automatic daily rolling backups stored safely on your computer. The system keeps thirty days of checksum-verified copies. School administrators can also export database backups to an external USB flash drive with one click for total disaster recovery protection.

Can schools import existing student data from Excel spreadsheets?

Yes. EduCore includes a fast Excel import tool for student admissions and staff records. Office staff can upload existing class rosters directly into the system. The built-in validator prevents duplicate roll numbers and flags missing fields before saving records.

Quick Setup Guide

How to Install EduCore Offline School ERP on Windows

Set up EduCore on your Windows 10 or 11 computer in three simple steps with complete local data security.

  1. Download the Setup Archive: Get the official Setup_EduCore_App.zip package directly from our website or GitHub releases.
  2. Extract the Installer Files: Right-click the downloaded zip archive in Windows Explorer and select Extract All to open the setup folder.
  3. Confirm Windows SmartScreen: Run EduCore_Setup_v8.2.3.exe. If prompted, click More info and choose Run anyway to proceed safely.
  4. Complete Installation: Follow the on-screen setup prompts, click Finish, and launch EduCore directly from your desktop icon.
