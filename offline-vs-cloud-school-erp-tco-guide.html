DPDP Act Readiness & Student Data Custody

School databases contain highly sensitive personal data: student photographs, dates of birth, guardian phone numbers, residential addresses, staff salary slips, and fee ledgers. Under India's Digital Personal Data Protection (DPDP) Act principles, schools acting as data fiduciaries carry direct accountability when transmitting records to external cloud SaaS vendors.

  • Zero Third-Party Cloud Exposure: Records never leave your campus PC unless an authorized administrator manually exports a local backup or Excel report.
  • Role-Based School ERP Login: Separate access controls for Master Administrators, accountants, and teachers with hashed credentials and tamper-evident audit logs.
  • 30-Day Integrity-Verified Backups: Automatic local snapshots run integrity checks (`PRAGMA quick_check`) and support 1-click USB flash drive disaster recovery.
