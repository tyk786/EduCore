Licensing & Ownership FAQs

Are any features or modules locked in lower tiers like Basic or Standard?

No! Every single tier in EduCore includes 100% of all ERP features and modules without any artificial feature gating. The only cap applied across tiers is the active student enrollment limit. Every school gets complete access to admissions, attendance, fees, payroll, exams, marksheets, ID cards, certificates, timetables, and library management.

How does offline license activation work?

An internet connection is required only once for a brief initial license verification. After entering your cryptographic license key, EduCore runs 100% offline without requiring internet access for your daily school operations.

Can we upgrade our tier as student enrollment expands?

Yes! You can upgrade between any tiers (e.g., from Basic to Standard or Enterprise) at any time simply by paying the tier difference. Since all features are already unlocked, all student records, fee ledgers, and exam data continue working smoothly without disruption.

What is included in the annual maintenance renewal?

The annual maintenance renewal provides access to all software updates, continuous feature enhancements, board report formatting updates, and priority customer support.

Is our school data accessible if we do not renew maintenance?

Yes. Unlike cloud subscription software that locks you out of your data, EduCore ensures perpetual data access. Your software continues to operate offline with complete access to all historical records.

How do we migrate our license to a new PC?

Simply create a backup archive within EduCore, install the software on your new Windows PC, restore the backup, and email us your new Machine ID for free license re-keying.
