🔑 How to Activate Your EduCore License

EduCore uses a hardware-locked licensing protocol that binds to your local Windows computer for offline security:

  1. Install and open EduCore on your primary administrative desktop or laptop.
  2. Navigate to Settings → License Management to view your unique Machine ID (e.g. WIN-XXXXXXXXXXXX).
  3. Email your Machine ID along with your desired tier (Trial, Basic, Standard, Professional, Enterprise, Diamond, Ultimate) to info@educoreapp.in.
  4. You will receive your cryptographic license activation key. Enter the key in the license dialog to permanently activate your system.
💡 Technical Troubleshooting Tip: When reaching out for technical diagnostics or database assistance, you can attach the educore_audit.log file located in your application folder to help us analyze and resolve inquiries swiftly.
